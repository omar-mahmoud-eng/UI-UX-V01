# Storefront UX acceptance checklist

- [ ] RTL and LTR are semantically correct including mixed Arabic/English numbers.
- [ ] Product + engraving/bouquet personalization flows show pricing and lead time before cart.
- [ ] Buyer billing and recipient delivery privacy are visibly separated.
- [ ] Gift message, hidden-price slip and anonymous mode are explicit and editable.
- [ ] Out-of-zone and unavailable delivery slots have honest useful recovery.
- [ ] Flower substitution requires understandable customer consent; no silent substitution.
- [ ] Mobile sticky CTA never obscures form validation, cookie notice or error.
- [ ] Guest checkout and account checkout show same essential options.
- [ ] Return and cancellation UX reflect policy-specific exceptions for custom/perishable products.
- [ ] All screens are free of lorem ipsum, fake promises, artificial stock/review deception.
